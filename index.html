❓ PathBack System Documentation & AI Pipeline

Comprehensive guide on biometric algorithms, FAISS indexing, and deployment

1. YuNet Face Detection

YuNet is a lightweight, edge-optimized convolutional neural network producing high-precision bounding boxes and 5 facial landmark keypoints under extreme lighting and angle variations.

2. SFace Deep Metric Recognition (128-D)

Extracts strictly L2-normalized 128-dimensional biometric embeddings. When normalized to unit Euclidean norm, dot product is mathematically equivalent to Cosine Similarity.

3. FAISS Vector Search (IndexFlatIP & IndexHNSWFlat)

Powers sub-millisecond similarity lookups comparing tens of thousands of missing person records against public tips with automatic fallback to vectorized NumPy dot products.
